How to Tell if Your Old Saree is Pure Silk or a Mix

By Muthu Praveena · Aug 29, 2025 · 4 min read

For many families, a silk saree is more than just clothing — it is a memory, an heirloom, and sometimes even an investment. But if you are planning to sell or pass it on, the first question to ask is: How to Tell if Your Saree is Pure Silk or a Mix? The value of your saree depends on the purity of the silk and the authenticity of the zari work.

At Om Sai Ram Pattu Center, we meet countless customers who aren't sure about their saree's material. As trusted old silk saree buyers in Chennai, we always guide them on how to tell if your Old saree is pure silk or a mix before selling.

Why Purity Matters

Knowing how to tell if your Old saree is pure silk or a mix is important because buyers calculate the price based on silk quality and zari content. Pure silk sarees with real zari fetch much higher rates compared to mixed or synthetic fabrics. This is why old pattu saree buyers in Chennai, secondhand silk saree buyers, and old zari buyers always check purity before offering a price.

5 Simple Ways: How to Tell if Your Old Saree is Pure Silk or a Mix

1. Feel the Fabric

The most common way in how to tell if your saree is pure silk or a mix is by touch. Pure silk feels soft yet has a slightly crisp, textured finish. Synthetic blends feel too smooth or slippery. Experienced old silk saree buyers in Chennai can often tell by touch alone.

2. Check the Zari

Look at the zari in the border and pallu. In the test of how to tell if your saree is pure silk or a mix, zari plays a big role. Real silver zari often shows a reddish or coppery shine on the reverse side, while imitation zari looks shiny yellow all through.

3. Burn Test (Only on a Loose Thread)

One traditional method of how to tell if your saree is pure silk or a mix is the burn test. Pure silk burns slowly, smells like burning hair, and turns to fine ash. Synthetic blends melt into hard beads with a plastic-like smell.

4. The Ring Test

Another quick way of how to tell if your saree is pure silk or a mix is the ring test. Try passing the saree through a small ring. Pure silk glides smoothly but with slight resistance, while synthetics either slip too fast or get stuck.

Many second hand pattu saree buyers in Chennai and old zari buyers also use traditional testing methods to ensure accuracy.
