Why Silver Zari Old Sarees Adds High Value?

In traditional weaving, silver zari is made from pure silver threads, sometimes coated with gold for added shine. This is very different from imitation zari made of synthetic materials or copper wire. For genuine old silk saree buyers, silver zari means:

  • Higher Resale Value – Sarees with real silver zari command better prices from old pattu saree buyers in Chennai.
  • Long-Lasting Quality – Silver zari maintains its shine for decades without losing its beauty.
  • Artisan Craftsmanship – Many vintage sarees with silver zari are handwoven and unique, which old zari buyers especially value. If you are considering selling, you'll find that old zari buyers in Chennai often purchase sarees even if the fabric is slightly damaged, simply for the value of the zari.

How to Check if Your Old Silk Saree Has Real Silver Zari

While professional old silk saree buyers like us use specific testing methods, you can try these simple checks at home:

  1. Flip the Saree – Look at the reverse side of the zari border. Real silver zari will show a reddish or coppery-white shine due to oxidation over time.
  2. Touch and Feel – Genuine zari feels smooth, soft, and slightly cool, unlike fake zari which feels rough or plasticky.
  3. Burn Test – If you have a small loose thread from the zari, burn it carefully. Real silver will not burn like synthetic fibres but will turn into fine ash. Remember: These are only basic tests.
